In Loving Memory of Donna

A faithful heart, a beautiful soul,
Now resting in God's grace.
Our Donna left a legacy
That time cannot erase.

Thomas, Jason, Jeremy, and Joy,
Stood by her, strong and true.
They carry on her kindness,
And the brightest parts she knew.

So fly high, Donna, into glory,
Your heavy burdens cease.
We lift our hearts and watch you rise
In everlasting peace.
